topic: Soil organic carbon and soil health
---
Soil organic carbon is a key component of soil organic matter and is central to soil health. Maintaining or increasing soil organic carbon can improve soil structure, biological activity and the capacity of soil to retain water and nutrients. Soil organic carbon is affected by land management, climate and land-use change. FAO's Global Soil Partnership treats soil organic carbon as an important indicator for monitoring sustainable soil management and climate mitigation. The local response to a practice depends on soil, climate and management context.

Measures that retain plant residues, reduce unnecessary soil disturbance and diversify vegetation can help maintain soil organic carbon where they are suitable to local conditions. Soil data should be interpreted with local agronomic and ecological knowledge.
