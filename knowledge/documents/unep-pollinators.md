---
The assessment on pollinators identifies land-use change, intensive agricultural management, pesticides, pests and pathogens, and climate change as important direct drivers affecting pollinators. Diverse habitats and floral resources in and around agricultural areas can support pollinator communities. Effects depend on landscape context and management.

Improving habitat quality and continuity can be considered alongside changes to farm management. Actions should reduce risks without introducing unsupported claims about the magnitude of pollinator recovery at a specific site.
