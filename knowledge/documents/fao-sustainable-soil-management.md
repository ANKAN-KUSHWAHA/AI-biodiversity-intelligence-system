FAO's voluntary guidelines describe sustainable soil management as maintaining or improving soil functions. Recommended principles include minimizing soil disturbance, maintaining soil cover, increasing biodiversity, and using crop rotations and associations adapted to local conditions. Soil cover can reduce erosion and support infiltration; diversified rotations and associated crops can support soil biological processes.

Management must account for water availability, climate, soil condition and local constraints. The guidelines do not imply that one practice has the same result in every location; monitoring soil organic carbon, soil cover, erosion and water-related indicators is important.
