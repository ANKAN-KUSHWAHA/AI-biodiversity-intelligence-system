topic: Deforestation, habitat fragmentation and restoration
---
Forest and landscape restoration aims to regain ecological functionality and improve human well-being across deforested or degraded landscapes. Restoring and protecting native vegetation can improve habitat condition and landscape connectivity. Fragmentation can isolate habitats, so landscape planning should consider connections between remaining patches.

Restoration priorities, native species and safeguards should be determined with local communities and ecological information. Monitoring habitat condition and biodiversity indicators is necessary to evaluate progress.
