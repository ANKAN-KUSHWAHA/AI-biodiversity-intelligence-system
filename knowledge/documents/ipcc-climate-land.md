topic: Climate impacts, water availability and land degradation
---
The IPCC Special Report on Climate Change and Land finds that land degradation and climate change can reinforce each other. Changes in precipitation and rising temperatures can increase pressure on dryland systems and affect water availability. Sustainable land management can contribute to adaptation, but outcomes vary with biophysical conditions and implementation.

Land management options should be evaluated in context, including climate risks, water constraints, food production and ecosystem services. Avoiding land degradation and maintaining vegetation cover can support resilience, while site-level monitoring is needed to establish local effects.
